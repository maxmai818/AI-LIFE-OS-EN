AI Life OS · G2 Prototypes

Three design directions, designed for a 375px phone screen. They are interactive: switch between "Capture → Confirm → Five Dims → Review" at the bottom. Once you have decided, reply with the variant number + your annotations.

Variant 1 · Information-Dense

A Linear/Things-style precision dashboard: a five-dimension overview on one screen, a structured entry stream, and a review with an evidence chain and confidence levels.

For: people who like a data feel and scanning everything at a glance

Variant 2 · Conversational-Guided

Like chatting with a trusted coach: quick capture is just sending a message, structured cards are confirmed in place, and the review is a guided conversation.

For: zero-friction capture, people who hate forms

Variant 3 · Card-Stack

A lightweight card flow: quick capture in a big input, confirmation feels like swiping cards, and the review is a stack of cards you flip through — five minutes and you are done.

For: a light, quick feel and gesture control

Comparison dimensions: positioning · strengths · main trade-offs — see the repo's WORK-LOG and each variant's NOTES.md. All demo data is fictional.
