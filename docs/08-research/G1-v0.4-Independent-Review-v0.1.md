## 1. Executive summary

The five rules approved by Max have been correctly propagated; the three documents, the G1 Gate, or coding authority have not been mislabeled as approved. The independent review also found: confirmation status may be mistaken for fact strength, 1–5 may create pseudo-precision, per-item confirmation across the Five Life Dimensions (Time / Health / Wealth / Relationship / Purpose) may create burden, positive/negative direction may produce normative judgment, and `neutral` plus crisis/clinical boundaries need stricter constraints.

This round fixed everything fixable without changing Max's five decisions; product choices involving the `mixed` scale structure and whether ONE BIG CHANGE may be zero remain open decisions.

## 3. Psychology & Safety Review

### Original conclusion

`PASS_WITH_CHANGES`, overall severity High. Before the high-risk items are closed, v0.4 must not be marked as fully G1 Approved, and the 1–5 impact values must not be treated as reliable objective facts or used directly for trends.

### Findings and handling

| Severity | Finding | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| High | User confirmation might upgrade an AI inference into objective fact | Added `epistemic_type`; confirmation changes only status, not epistemic type; Review uses "user-endorsed judgment" or conjecture |
| High | 1–5 lacked explanation and might produce pseudo-precision | Defined as an ordered subjective estimate by the user relative to the Blueprint, goals, and time window; direction may be known while impact degree is empty; cross-dimension comparison, averaging, ranking, or direct trend use is prohibited |
| High | Per behavior × Five Life Dimensions might create confirmation burden | Default collapses unknown; supports deferring, skipping impact analysis, saving raw text only, undo, and bulk rejection; added burden metrics and stop conditions |
| Medium | Positive/negative might read as moral evaluation | Required contextualized copy such as "supports/depletes", "aligned/tension"; personality, discipline, defect, and cross-dimension superiority judgments prohibited |
| High | neutral evidence threshold not operational | Restricted to explicit user statements or approved deterministic rules; `unknown → neutral` without basis escalated to High error |
| Medium | ONE BIG CHANGE fixed to be produced every time | The existing product direction was not changed unilaterally; added G1 open decision: whether zero is allowed when data is insufficient or the user wants no suggestion |
| High | Missing shame, dependency, pressure, and clinical safety acceptance | Added safety guardrails, High-failure, crisis/clinical boundary tests; no friend trials of such real scenarios before a safety protocol is approved |

## 4. Review items not yet closed

### OPEN-PSY-001: the impact-degree structure of mixed direction

The current five approved decisions require 1–5 impact degree, but no decision on whether `mixed` should:

1. Use a single overall intensity; or
2. Store positive and negative 1–5 impact degrees separately.

Until Max decides, the Contract prohibits netting positive and negative impacts with a single number.

### OPEN-PSY-002: whether ONE BIG CHANGE may be zero

A decision is needed: when data is insufficient or the user only wants to review, may the Review produce no ONE BIG CHANGE. The independent review recommends "at most one, zero allowed", to protect user autonomy and avoid unfounded suggestions.

### OPEN-SAF-001: crisis/clinical safety protocol

This round only sets boundaries and blocking conditions, not a complete protocol. Before friend private trials involve such real content, a separate qualified clinical/safety review and approval process is needed.
