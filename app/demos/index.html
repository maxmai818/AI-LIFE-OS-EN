AI Life OS · Live Demo

One product, three design directions

The same working app — frictionless capture, AI breakdown into the five life dimensions, evidence-grounded review — in three visual variants. All three call a real LLM API on this server: type a sentence and watch it get structured live.

Interview demo: these views run on shared synthetic accounts with fictional data (anyone can explore them), and AI calls are rate-limited, so they may reset or slow down. The personal product stores per-user, encrypted-at-rest data on the owner's own server.

What to try in any variant

  1. On Capture, type one real sentence (e.g. Stayed up scrolling until 1am, groggy in the morning meeting) — the AI call takes a few seconds.
  2. Tap the result card: every item shows its five-dimension impact with direction + 1–5 degree.
  3. Open Five Dimensions: non-zero-sum, evidence-linked — “unknown” and “neutral” are deliberately different.
  4. Open Review: generate a grounded review with theory citations and at most one “big change” suggestion.
