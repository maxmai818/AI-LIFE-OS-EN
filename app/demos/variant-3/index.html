Your data, your call

Everything lives in one SQLite file on your own server — copy the file to export it, delete the file to delete everything. The original text of every capture is kept exactly as you wrote it; corrections are stored as revisions beside the AI's original values.

Privacy & AI boundaries: a cloud LLM may process the content of your entries while extraction runs. AI output is provisional until you confirm it.
